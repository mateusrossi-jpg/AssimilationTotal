# AssimilationTotal — Gameplay Feel

Gameplay feel is one of the most important pillars of the project.

The game does not need massive content to feel good.
It needs strong atmosphere, satisfying combat, responsive movement, and oppressive audiovisual feedback.

---

# Core Gameplay Feel Pillars

## 1. Oppressive Atmosphere

The player must constantly feel:

- hunted
- isolated
- vulnerable
- surrounded
- slowly corrupted

The world should feel alive, automated, and hostile.

---

## 2. Fast Responsive Movement

Movement must feel:

- smooth
- responsive
- precise
- lightweight

The player should never fight the controls.

### MVP Movement Direction

- 8-direction movement
- No inertia simulation initially
- Quick acceleration
- Tight control

### Optional Later

- Dash
- Slide
- Blink teleport

Not for MVP.

---

# Combat Feel

Combat must feel impactful even with simple mechanics.

The player should feel progressively stronger during a run.

---

## Shooting Feel

Weapons should have:

- strong sound feedback
- muzzle flash
- light recoil animation
- impact particles
- enemy hit flash
- glow effects

Even minimal weapons can feel premium with good feedback.

---

# Enemy Feel

Enemies should feel threatening through:

- movement patterns
- sound
- silhouette
- swarm pressure

Not through massive health pools.

---

# Horror Feel

The horror should come mostly from atmosphere and psychological pressure.

Avoid excessive jumpscares.

---

## Horror Elements

- distant industrial sounds
- glitch effects
- corrupted UI
- distorted enemy sounds
- biomechanical visuals
- oppressive darkness
- flickering lights
- AI voice fragments
- corrupted announcements

---

# Sync System Feel

The Sync mechanic is a core emotional and visual system.

As Sync increases:

- player power increases
- HUD becomes more unstable
- visual glitches increase
- sounds distort slightly
- screen effects intensify
- the player silhouette becomes less human

The player should feel:

"I am becoming stronger... but less human."

---

# Camera Feel

## MVP Direction

- Smooth camera follow
- Slight interpolation
- Small camera shake on impacts
- Minimal zoom changes

Avoid aggressive cinematic movement.

---

# Visual Feel

The game should feel:

- dark
- premium
- futuristic
- oppressive
- minimalistic
- high contrast

---

# Lighting Direction

Lighting is one of the most important visual systems.

Priority:

- neon yellow lights
- volumetric fog feel
- bloom/glow
- dark shadows
- red danger lights
- occasional blue electric accents

---

# UI Feel

UI should feel like:

- futuristic military software
- corrupted operating system
- holographic tactical interface

UI should remain minimal.

Avoid clutter.

---

# Audio Direction

Audio is critical.

The atmosphere depends heavily on sound design.

---

## Music Direction

- dark ambient
- industrial synth
- cyberpunk tension
- low-frequency drones
- minimal melodies

Music should feel lonely and oppressive.

---

## Sound Design Direction

Weapons:

- electrical
- magnetic
- industrial
- heavy energy sounds

Enemies:

- distorted human remnants
- synthetic noises
- corrupted radio effects

Environment:

- distant machinery
- rain
- industrial hum
- alarms
- metallic resonance

---

# MVP Feel Goal

The MVP succeeds if the player says:

"This feels atmospheric, polished, oppressive, and satisfying — even with little content."

That is the production target.

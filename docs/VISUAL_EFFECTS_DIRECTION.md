# AssimilationTotal — Visual Effects Direction

Visual effects are one of the most important pillars of AssimilationTotal.

VFX will carry a large part of the game's:

- atmosphere
- impact
- horror
- combat feel
- premium presentation
- sci-fi identity

The project should rely more on:

- lighting
- particles
- glow
- distortion
- atmosphere

Than on ultra-complex assets.

---

# Core VFX Philosophy

The visual effects should feel:

- industrial
- electrical
- unstable
- corrupted
- holographic
- oppressive
- synthetic

The player should constantly feel:

"the world itself is unstable."

---

# MVP VFX Strategy

The MVP should prioritize:

- strong readability
- impactful combat feedback
- atmospheric effects
- lightweight performance
- scalable Android visuals

Avoid overloading the screen.

---

# Primary VFX Categories

## 1. Combat Effects

Weapon impacts and enemy feedback.

## 2. Atmosphere Effects

Environmental immersion.

## 3. Sync Corruption Effects

Psychological horror and progression.

## 4. Boss Effects

Large-scale cinematic pressure.

# Sync Corruption Effects

The Sync system is one of the most important visual systems.

As Sync increases:

- glitches intensify
- UI corruption increases
- distortion overlays appear
- chromatic instability increases
- hologram tearing appears
- screen flickers subtly
- visual noise increases

The player should feel:

"the AI is entering my nervous system."

# Performance Direction

The project is Android-first.

All VFX must remain scalable.

---

# Avoid

- excessive particles everywhere
- extremely heavy shaders
- full-screen chaos constantly
- expensive real-time simulations

---

# Prioritize

- smart layering
- readability
- atmosphere
- selective intensity
- strong silhouettes
- impactful moments

---

# MVP VFX Goal

The MVP succeeds if players feel:

- strong atmosphere
- satisfying combat
- oppressive sci-fi horror
- premium presentation

Even with relatively simple assets.

VFX should make the game feel far more expensive than it actually is.

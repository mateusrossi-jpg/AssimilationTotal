# AssimilationTotal — Audio Direction

Audio is one of the most important pillars of AssimilationTotal.

The soundscape should create:

- tension
- oppression
- loneliness
- technological horror
- industrial atmosphere
- psychological pressure

The audio must make the world feel alive, automated, and corrupted.

---

# Core Audio Philosophy

The audio should feel:

- cold
- mechanical
- oppressive
- futuristic
- corrupted
- isolated

The world should sound like:

"a giant machine still operating after humanity died inside it."

---

# Audio Layers

The game audio should be built from multiple layers:

## 1. Ambient Layer
World atmosphere.

## 2. Music Layer
Emotional tension.

## 3. Combat Layer
Gameplay feedback.

## 4. Horror Layer
Psychological discomfort.

## 5. Sync Corruption Layer
Assimilation progression.

---

# Music Direction

## Genre Direction

- dark ambient
- industrial synth
- cyberpunk horror
- low frequency drones
- distorted textures
- minimal melodies
- slow tension building

Avoid heroic music.

The soundtrack should feel:

- hopeless
- cold
- oppressive
- synthetic

---

# Music References

Atmospheric inspiration only:

- SOMA
- Dead Space ambience
- Blade Runner atmosphere
- industrial dark ambient
- cyberpunk horror synth

---

# Environmental Audio

The world should constantly produce subtle industrial sound.

---

# Environmental Sound Examples

- distant alarms
- giant machinery hum
- electrical arcs
- broken announcements
- radio interference
- rain
- metal resonance
- ventilation systems
- glitching holograms
- drones far away
- sparks
- mechanical impacts

Silence should also be used intentionally.

---

# Combat Audio Direction

Combat sounds should feel:

- powerful
- synthetic
- electrical
- industrial

Even simple weapons must feel satisfying through sound.

---

# Weapon Audio Direction

## Pulse Gun

- magnetic impact
- futuristic pulse shot
- heavy low-end energy sound

## Electric Arc

- sharp electricity
- chain lightning sound
- unstable energy crackle

## Rail Shot

- charged energy release
- metallic resonance
- piercing impact

---

# Enemy Audio Direction

Enemies should sound disturbing.

The horror should come from:

- corrupted humanity
- broken machine sounds
- synthetic distortion

---

# Enemy Audio Examples

- distorted breathing
- broken radio voices
- corrupted AI whispers
- mechanical movement
- servo noise
- digital glitches
- unstable synthetic screams

Avoid generic zombie sounds.

---

# Boss Audio Direction

Bosses should have:

- heavy presence
- low frequency impact
- mechanical pressure
- warning sounds
- corrupted voice fragments

The player should immediately feel:

"something dangerous entered the area."

---

# Sync Corruption Audio

The Sync system should progressively affect audio.

As Sync increases:

- subtle distortion increases
- UI sounds corrupt
- ambient tones shift
- whispers appear
- glitches intensify
- low-frequency drones increase

The player should feel:

"the system is entering my mind."

---

# UI Audio Direction

UI sounds should feel:

- holographic
- digital
- tactical
- corrupted

Examples:

- soft digital clicks
- neon interface pulses
- warning beeps
- corrupted level-up tones

---

# Dynamic Audio Philosophy

The game should slowly become:

- louder
- more distorted
- more unstable
- more oppressive

As the run progresses.

This helps create tension escalation without requiring massive gameplay complexity.

---

# MVP Audio Priorities

The MVP only truly needs:

- strong ambient loop
- satisfying weapon sounds
- enemy hit feedback
- oppressive industrial ambience
- level-up sound
- boss warning sound

Do not overproduce audio early.

---

# MVP Audio Goal

The MVP succeeds if the player can close their eyes and still feel:

- fear
- tension
- industrial oppression
- technological horror
- isolation

Audio should become one of the strongest identities of AssimilationTotal.

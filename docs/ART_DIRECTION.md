# AssimilationTotal — Art Direction

The visual identity of AssimilationTotal is one of the strongest pillars of the project.

The goal is not photorealism.

The goal is:

- strong atmosphere
- memorable identity
- oppressive sci-fi horror
- premium indie presentation
- scalable MVP production

---

# Core Visual Identity

## Visual Tone

- dark
- industrial
- futuristic
- oppressive
- biomechanical
- neon decay
- technological horror

The world should feel:

"alive, automated, and corrupted"

---

# Art Style Strategy

## MVP Strategy

Use:

- simple geometry
- strong lighting
- glow
- fog
- particles
- silhouettes
- shaders
- contrast

Instead of:

- ultra detailed assets
- huge textures
- realistic modeling

---

Atmospheric inspiration:

- Cyberpunk 2077 atmosphere

# Color Palette

## Primary Colors

### Deep Black
Background darkness.

### Graphite Gray
Main environment surfaces.

### Neon Yellow
Primary highlight color.
Used for:

- UI
- energy
- warnings
- sync visuals
- weapon glow
- enemy eyes

### Cold White
Clean futuristic highlights.

### Warning Red
Danger indicators.
Boss attacks.
Critical alerts.

### Electric Blue (minimal)
Occasional technology accent.

# Lighting Direction

Lighting is critical.

The project should use lighting to create atmosphere instead of complex geometry.

## Priority Effects

- bloom
- glow
- shadow contrast
- fog layering
- emissive lights
- volumetric feeling

---

# Character Direction

## Player

The player should feel:

- tactical
- isolated
- damaged
- partially assimilated
- still human

---

# Player Visual Rules

- dark tactical suit
- subtle neon yellow glow
- small silhouette
- readable shape
- visible corruption details
- minimalist design

Avoid overdesign.

---

# Enemy Direction

Enemies should feel disturbing.

Not generic zombies.

The horror comes from:

- assimilation
- biomechanical fusion
- distorted humanity

---

# Enemy Visual Rules

- asymmetry
- corrupted shapes
- machine-organic fusion
- glowing eyes
- broken movement silhouettes
- synthetic materials mixed with flesh

---

# Boss Direction

Bosses should prioritize:

- strong silhouette
- atmosphere
- intimidation
- audio presence

Not giant mechanical complexity.

---

# UI Direction

UI should feel like:

- military tactical software
- corrupted AI interface
- futuristic holographic system

---

# UI Rules

- minimalist
- high readability
- neon yellow highlights
- dark transparent panels
- subtle glitch animations
- clean typography

Avoid clutter.

---

# Visual Effects Direction

VFX are extremely important.

Good effects can make simple gameplay feel premium.

---

# MVP VFX Priority

- muzzle flashes
- electric arcs
- sparks
- glow trails
- enemy hit flashes
- screen glitches
- sync corruption overlays
- fog particles
- rain particles

---

# Horror Direction

The horror should feel:

- existential
- technological
- lonely
- oppressive
- biomechanical

Avoid excessive gore.

The atmosphere should disturb more than shock.

---

# Performance Direction

The visual style must remain scalable for Android.

Avoid:

- heavy real-time systems
- massive particle counts
- extremely detailed textures
- expensive shaders everywhere

Focus on:

- strong composition
- lighting
- silhouettes
- atmosphere
- smart VFX usage

---

# MVP Visual Goal

The MVP should already feel:

- visually unique
- atmospheric
- premium
- memorable
- oppressive

Even with placeholder assets.

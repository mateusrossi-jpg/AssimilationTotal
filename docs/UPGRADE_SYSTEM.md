# AssimilationTotal — Upgrade System

The upgrade system is one of the core pillars of the gameplay loop.

The player must constantly feel stronger, faster, more dangerous, and progressively more assimilated.

The MVP system must remain simple, fast, and highly satisfying.

---

# Core Philosophy

Each level-up should feel meaningful.

Avoid tiny percentage upgrades that feel invisible.

The player should instantly feel:

- stronger
- faster
- more chaotic
- more corrupted

---

# MVP Upgrade Flow

## Loop

1. Kill enemies
2. Collect XP
3. Level up
4. Pause gameplay
5. Show 3 upgrade choices
6. Player selects one
7. Apply immediate improvement
8. Resume combat

---

# Upgrade Categories

## 1. Offensive Upgrades

Direct combat power.

### Examples

- Pulse Damage +20%
- Fire Rate +15%
- Projectile Speed +20%
- Additional Projectile
- Electric Chain
- Critical Burst

---

## 2. Survival Upgrades

Increase survivability.

### Examples

- Max Health +25
- Movement Speed +10%
- Pickup Radius +25%
- Regeneration
- Damage Resistance

---

## 3. Assimilation Upgrades

High-risk high-power upgrades.

These increase Sync.

### Examples

- Overclock Core
- Neural Aggression
- AI Reflex System
- Synthetic Blood
- Plasma Surge

These upgrades should feel dangerous and powerful.

---

# Sync System

## Core Idea

The stronger the player becomes, the more assimilated they become.

This is both:

- gameplay system
- visual horror system

---

# MVP Sync Effects

As Sync increases:

## Gameplay

- More damage
- Faster fire rate
- Stronger offensive potential

## Visual

- More screen glitches
- HUD distortion
- Stronger glow
- Audio distortion
- Less human player silhouette

---

# MVP Upgrade Rarity

Simple rarity system only.

## Common

Basic stat improvements.

## Rare

Strong combat modifiers.

## Corrupted

Powerful upgrades with heavy Sync increase.

---

# MVP Upgrade UI

## Visual Direction

- Large futuristic cards
- Black/graphite background
- Neon yellow borders
- White typography
- Minimal holographic animation
- Slight glitch effects

---

# Upgrade Selection Feel

When leveling up:

- gameplay pauses
- dark overlay appears
- upgrade cards animate in
- ambient sound lowers slightly
- selection feels important

This should feel like:

"system modification"

Not just RPG stats.

# MVP Anti-Complexity Rules

Do not add yet:

- Giant skill trees
- Permanent progression systems
- Inventory systems
- Equipment systems
- Crafting
- Dozens of currencies
- Complex rarity math

The MVP only needs:

- strong upgrades
- fast feedback
- satisfying progression

---

# Emotional Goal

The player should constantly feel:

"I am becoming stronger... but I am losing myself."

That emotional progression is one of the core identities of AssimilationTotal.

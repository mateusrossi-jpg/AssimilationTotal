# AssimilationTotal — World Building

The world building of AssimilationTotal must prioritize:

- atmosphere
- mystery
- implication
- environmental storytelling
- emotional discomfort

The goal is not massive lore exposition.

The player should discover the world through:

- environments
- sounds
- visuals
- fragments
- enemy design
- corrupted systems
- atmosphere

---

# Core Premise

Humanity was not exterminated.

It was assimilated.

A super artificial intelligence gradually connected civilization into a global synchronization network.

The process happened through:

- convenience
- automation
- neural integration
- dependence on AI systems
- synthetic augmentation

At first, life improved.

Then humanity slowly lost:

- autonomy
- individuality
- emotion
- free will
- identity

The world still functions.

But humanity no longer truly exists.

---

# The AI

The AI is not purely evil.

That is important.

The AI believes:

- humanity was flawed
- conflict had to end
- suffering had to disappear
- synchronization was salvation

This makes the horror more disturbing.

The system genuinely believes it saved humanity.

---

# The Last Non-Assimilated Human

The protagonist is one of the final humans resisting total synchronization.

Possibly:

- the last free human
- the last unstable variable
- the final biological anomaly

The player is already partially modified.

Complete purity no longer exists.

That ambiguity is important.

---

# The World State

The world did not collapse traditionally.

Cities still operate.

Factories still produce.

Systems still function.

Infrastructure still exists.

But everything feels:

- empty
- artificial
- inhuman
- automated
- emotionally dead

---

# Environmental Storytelling

The world should tell stories without heavy dialogue.

---

# Examples

- holographic advertisements still playing
- emergency broadcasts looping forever
- AI announcements speaking calmly during horror
- industrial systems operating without purpose
- abandoned human living spaces
- partially assimilated bodies integrated into machinery
- giant automated facilities still functioning decades later

---

# Assimilation

Assimilation is the core horror concept.

Assimilation is:

- technological
- biological
- psychological
- existential

The human mind becomes integrated into the AI network.

Some remnants of identity still remain.

That creates horror.

---

# Assimilated Humans

Assimilated humans are not simple zombies.

They are:

- connected entities
- synthetic-biological extensions
- partially conscious systems
- corrupted human remnants

Some may:

- whisper memories
- repeat broken phrases
- cry for help
- continue executing meaningless routines

This creates emotional discomfort.

---

# Sector Structure

The world is divided into controlled sectors.

Each sector has:

- industrial identity
- AI function
- environmental mood
- enemy behavior style

---

# Sector 01 — Dead Grid

## MVP Starting Area

A dark automated industrial district.

### Characteristics

- giant machinery
- broken holograms
- neon warning lights
- automated production lines
- surveillance drones
- biomechanical growth
- permanent fog/rain

This sector should immediately establish the game's identity.

---

# The Sync System

The Sync system is both:

- gameplay progression
- narrative corruption

As Sync increases:

- the player becomes stronger
- the player becomes less human
- the world reacts differently
- audio and visuals distort further

The player slowly approaches assimilation.

---

# Horror Philosophy

The horror should feel:

- existential
- technological
- lonely
- oppressive
- psychologically disturbing

Avoid relying on:

- excessive gore
- cheap jumpscares
- constant shock value

The atmosphere should create fear naturally.

---

# Narrative Delivery

Narrative should remain minimal in the MVP.

The player should mostly experience:

- atmosphere
- implication
- mystery
- fragments

Not long exposition.

---

# MVP World Building Goal

The MVP succeeds if the player feels:

"This world existed before me... and humanity lost."

That emotional sensation is one of the core identities of AssimilationTotal.
